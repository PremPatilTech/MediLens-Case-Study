> These personas are working assumptions. They must be checked in interviews before anyone treats them as facts.

## Persona 1: the territory sales rep

- **Context:** sells pharma or medical-supply products across a city or region; targets pharmacies, small clinics.
- **Goal:** a ready list of reachable accounts and a decent first message, today.
- **Frustration:** hours spent copying numbers from maps and websites.
- **Job to be done:** *"When I start working a new area, help me find and rank the accounts I can actually reach so I spend my day talking to people, not searching."*
- **App features that serve it:** map and radius search, fit score and tiers, bulk actions, export.

## Persona 2: the medical science liaison (MSL)

- **Context:** needs a credible, clinical tone with specialists and clinic directors.
- **Goal:** reach the right clinician with a message that reflects their practice.
- **Frustration:** generic outreach damages credibility.
- **Job to be done:** *"When I introduce a therapy or service to a clinic, help me address the right decision-maker with a message that fits their specialty."*
- **App features:** deep enrichment (decision-makers), liaison profiles with credentials and pitch, tone choice, personalisation score.

## Persona 3: the distributor owner

- **Context:** small supply business growing a customer list.
- **Goal:** steady new accounts without hiring a researcher.
- **Job to be done:** *"When I want more buyers, give me a repeatable way to find and contact new healthcare businesses nearby."*
- **App features:** repeat searches by sector, outreach pipeline, insights.

## Not served (yet)

Hospital procurement committees, regulated medical-device sales with compliance review, multi-user sales teams with shared pipelines.
