| Alternative | What it does well | Where a small team may find it hard | MediLens angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pharma CRM suites** (e.g. Veeva) | Compliance, field-force workflows, deep healthcare data | Cost and setup suit large companies | Lightweight, territory-first, no CRM migration |
| **Healthcare data providers** (e.g. IQVIA) | Verified clinician datasets | Subscription pricing; coverage varies by country | Builds a list from public web data on demand |
| **General B2B contact tools** (e.g. Apollo, ZoomInfo) | Large contact databases, sequences | Not tuned to clinics and pharmacies; local small businesses are thin | Healthcare categories and clinical-fit scoring |
| **Directories and maps** (Google Maps, local directories) | Free, broad local coverage | Manual; no contact enrichment or scoring | Automates the collection and ranking |
| **Spreadsheets and manual research** | Zero cost, full control | Slow, inconsistent | Same outcome in minutes |

## Takeaways

- The gap is **small teams that cannot buy enterprise data** and still want a clean, ranked, personalised workflow.
- The defensible part is the **combination** (discover, enrich, score, personalise, send), not any single step.
- Risk: data-protection and platform rules around contact data (see [`privacy-and-compliance.md`](../06-quality-launch/privacy-and-compliance.md)).

*Product names are used only for category comparison. No claims about their features or pricing are made beyond general public knowledge.*
