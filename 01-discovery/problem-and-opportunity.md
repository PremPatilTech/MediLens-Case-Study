## The problem

Reaching the right person at a healthcare business is slow and manual. A sales rep or medical liaison who wants to talk to pharmacies, clinics or distributors in a territory has to:

1. find the businesses (maps, directories),
2. open each website to hunt for an email, a phone number and the name of someone who decides,
3. judge which ones are worth contacting,
4. write a message that does not read like a mass email,
5. keep track of who was contacted.

Each step uses a different tool, and the work is repeated for every territory.

## Who feels it

Small and mid-size pharma and medical-supply sales teams, independent distributors and medical science liaisons, mostly working a geographic territory without a large data-subscription budget. *(Hypothesis: no interviews have been done yet.)*

## Opportunity

One workflow that goes from "show me healthcare businesses within 5 km" to "here is a reviewed, personalised message ready to send", with a score that says which accounts are reachable first.

## Why healthcare and not "all leads"

An earlier general-purpose prototype had no clear buyer. Healthcare gives sharper definitions: the account types (pharmacy, clinic, hospital, supply store), the decision-makers (clinic director, practice manager, lead physician) and the tone of outreach (clinical, consultative, direct) are specific, which makes both the data and the message better.

## What would prove the opportunity

See [`success-metrics.md`](../02-definition/success-metrics.md). In short: users complete a search-to-message flow without help, and the messages they send get replies.
